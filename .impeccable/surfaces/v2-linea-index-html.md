# Surface brief: v2 landing, direction "La linea unica" (v2/linea/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led. Sibling of v2/lucido (same copy, different world). v1 in the repo root is the reference for approved copy and is untouched.

## Audience and job
Same as PRODUCT.md. Ask from the owner for this version: Goodlearning simplifies, with precision and functions that used to cost a lot; the design must show it, not say it. Modern, airy, product-software feel, bold. Approved copy: the v1 text. Real product evidence on hand: one cropped GoodSuite screenshot (Marketplace view) and the three AI-module logos from goodlearning.it, with their provenance recorded.

## Scope
Single Italian page, static HTML/CSS/JS. Font self-hosted (Host Grotesk). Shared v2 assets in v2/assets. Contact form with endpoint hook and mailto fallback.

## Unresolved
Form endpoint; higher-resolution screenshots and logo; AI model and hosting claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One line. A web of thin grey routes, the old processes, merges into a single thick cobalt line, and that line is the spine of the whole page: every section is a station on it, and the page gets simpler as you travel. It refuses the split SaaS hero and rows of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; ink #0a1230; one line colour, cobalt #0a4fd6, at about 14px; grey routes #c3cadb at 3px; 45 degree bends with rounded corners; stations as white discs with a thick ring; Goodlearning yellow (#f0c010) only for the AI modules and the terminus. Host Grotesk bold, large and tight. Flat, no shadows; UI cards have a hairline border only. Recognizable with all copy removed: grey routes merging into one blue line with ringed stations.

STORY: The visitor watches many routes become one, rides the line past the real product, the proof and the plans, and arrives at the terminus where the contact form waits.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White. Two-line headline top-left. Below it a map band: grey routes enter from the left edge and merge at about 28% of the width into the thick line, which runs right through four stations (Documento, GoodContent AI, Corso, Registro) with small UI cards hanging from them, and ends at a terminus on the right where a compact form card (name, email, company, button) hangs. On load the routes draw in and then merge.

FORM: La linea unica (candidate 2 of 7, pick card, seed key 047b454b). Raises: ekiben kiosk, a printed contents map as the way in, so a compact index of stations sits in the nav; minihompy, small honest chrome, so the real numbers sit as plain counters on the line; Art Deco lobby, thresholds that tighten, so the line gets fewer branches and the layout simpler toward the terminus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
