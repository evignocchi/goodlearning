# Surface brief: landing home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine; not stored in config).

## Audience and job
HR Manager or CEO of an industrial/service company or a training provider, arriving from outbound or word of mouth to check what Goodlearning is. Action: send a contact request (form on the page). Proof: real numbers and four first-name testimonials from the live site, pricing facts from the pricing page. No invented customers, models, hosting or response-time claims.

## Scope
Single Italian landing page, static HTML/CSS/JS. Self-hosted Archivo variable font (no Google Fonts: GDPR). Contact form posts to a configurable endpoint; falls back to mailto: info@goodlearning.it. No cookies, no analytics, so no cookie banner.

## Unresolved
Form endpoint; higher-resolution or SVG logo (only a 190x31 PNG exists); AI model and hosting statements.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GoodSuite is a machine that turns company documents into trackable training; the page is that machine seen once, flat and frontal, in Italian industrial modernism. It refuses the SaaS hero of headline-left, dashboard-right, logo strip below.

OWN-WORLD: Electric cobalt field (#0a47c9, the logo's blue family pushed to full saturation) owning whole bands; cool white plates (#f2f5fb, #fff); deep ink blue (#071a45); one signal orange-red (#ff4a1c) reserved for the contact action and the paper in motion. Flat geometric forms only: sheet, rollers, belt, course slide, register page. Archivo variable at wide widths, lowercase display like the logo, tabular numerals. Recognizable with all copy removed: cobalt bands, a continuous belt line, orange paper.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport that a procedure goes in and a tracked course comes out, believes it because the numbers, plans and quotes are real and specific, and sends the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cobalt band under a white nameplate tab holding the logo. Lowercase headline across about 70% of the width at display size, three lines. Under it the belt spans the full width: document, rollers, course, register, each labelled in plain text beneath its drawing. The belt ends in the contact tray at bottom right: name, work email, company or body, orange "Richiedi contatto". Orange sheet travels the belt once on load.

FORM: Olivetti-era Italian industrial modernism (assigned candidate 5 of 7, seed key 6f4fd6c5), executed contemporary: oversized type, flat blocks, hard alignment, no retro texture.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
