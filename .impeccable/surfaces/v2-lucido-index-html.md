# Surface brief: v2 landing, direction "Il lucido" (v2/lucido/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led. Sibling of v2/linea (same copy, different world). v1 in the repo root is the reference for approved copy and is untouched.

## Audience and job
Same as PRODUCT.md. Ask from the owner for this version: Goodlearning simplifies, with precision and functions that used to cost a lot; the design must show it, not say it. Modern, airy, product-software feel, bold. Approved copy: the v1 text. Real product evidence on hand: one cropped GoodSuite screenshot (Marketplace view) and the three AI-module logos from goodlearning.it, with their provenance recorded.

## Scope
Single Italian page, static HTML/CSS/JS. Fonts self-hosted (Bricolage Grotesque, Geist). Shared v2 assets in v2/assets. Contact form with endpoint hook and mailto fallback.

## Unresolved
Form endpoint; higher-resolution screenshots and logo; AI model and hosting claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Simplification is shown as tracing. A grey underlay holds the tangle of the old process; one bold cobalt line keeps only what matters, and the page is the light table where that happens. It refuses the split SaaS hero and v1's heavy flat colour blocks.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light-table ground (#eef1f7); translucent vellum sheets (white at ~70%) that truly show and soften what lies under them; graphite hairlines (#8a94ab) for the mess; cobalt marker line (#0b57d0) for the essential; yellow highlighter (#f5c400, the Goodlearning AI yellow) for the one thing traced; ink #0b1430. Bricolage Grotesque display with Geist text. Sheets carry soft offset shadows. Recognizable with all copy removed: stacked frosted sheets and one thick blue stroke over a grey tangle.

STORY: The visitor sees mess become one clean line, believes it because real GoodSuite screens sit at the end of the line, and sends the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Table-grey ground, glass nav. Two-line headline with "tracciati" under a yellow highlighter stroke. Directly under it the lede and a compact three-field form with an ink button. Below, a full-width band: grey tangle on the left, a vellum sheet crossing it, the blue line traced across four labelled chips, ending in the real GoodSuite screenshot that bleeds off the bottom-right. On load the line draws itself and the tangle fades to about 40%.

FORM: Il lucido (assigned candidate 7 of 7, seed key 047b454b). Raises: Art Deco cinema lobby, the page tightens from wide to essential until the contact sheet; raku, the highlighter colour only on what is active now; gravity garden, everything moves except the contact action, which stays level.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
