## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user's choice). Single-page landing, Italian. The existing goodlearning.it site (Django/Wagtail-style, Bootstrap-based) stays as is; this landing is a rebuild of its home page only.

## Users

Primary buyers: HR Manager and CEO/owner. Two customer segments, both B2B only:
- **Aziende** (industrial and service companies) running continuous or compliance training for their staff.
- **Enti di formazione** (training providers) that deliver courses to their own learners.

Typical situation: someone responsible for training who needs one platform for synchronous, asynchronous and blended courses, with tracking that holds up with certifying bodies and funders.

## Product Purpose

Goodlearning sells **GoodSuite**, a proprietary cloud LMS (created by engineers, based in Mazzano, Brescia). Modules sit under it: GoodMeeting (web meeting with attendance tracking), GoodContent AI (documents to interactive courses with slides, quizzes, games and talking avatars), GoodAnswer AI (chatbot answering from the company's own knowledge), GoodSlides AI (presentations and speaker scripts from a document, PowerPoint export). The company positions the product as moving from "LMS" to a Knowledge Management System.

Success for this landing: **contact requests** (a form on the page). Free-plan signups are secondary.

## Positioning

An LMS that is also a KMS: courses, meetings, workflows and AI that answers from the company's own documents, in one system. Differentiators the user confirmed: deep customization (the customer's own logo and platform), responsive technical support, a simple course editor, and strength in mandatory-training and compliance contexts in Italy. Direct competitors are generic LMSs (Moodle, TalentLMS and Italian peers); do not name competitors on the page.

## Operating Context

- Revenue today is led by GoodSuite; other lines are custom projects (GoodIdea, GoodPlanning) and course creation services.
- About twenty paying customers. Acquisition is outbound plus word of mouth, so the landing is mostly visited by people who already heard of Goodlearning and want to verify it, or who arrive from outbound messages.
- Mandatory training (safety, 231, GDPR) and tracking toward certifying bodies matter in this market.

## Product Principles

1. One system, not a bundle: courses, meetings, workflows and AI answer the same need, the company's knowledge.
2. Say what it does in plain Italian, with the numbers the company can stand behind; no AI hype and no claims about models or hosting that are not confirmed.
3. Built for the person who has to defend the training record: tracking, reporting and compliance come before novelty.
4. Make the first contact easy: one short form, asked for early and again at the end.
5. Respect the buyer's time: HR managers and CEOs decide in seconds whether it is for them.
